Yocal

Your local guide to the River Yealm

Tides, weather, beaches, parking, walks and Cal, the assistant who knows the river. Made in Newton Ferrers.

Yocal is an iPhone app. Some of it is free for everyone, including SOS, today’s tide times, Local and the map. The rest is Yocal Plus, which you can try free for 2 hours.
